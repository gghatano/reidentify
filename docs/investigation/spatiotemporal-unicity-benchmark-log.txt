== 4. unicity is a lower bound, not an upper one ==
  unicity <= expected_id_rate in 64/64 rows
  largest gap 0.3034 at k = 1, time 1, space 10:
    unicity says 0.1089 of people are pinned down; an attacker
    guessing inside the anonymity set gets 0.4124 of them
  Reporting unicity alone would understate the risk by that much.
