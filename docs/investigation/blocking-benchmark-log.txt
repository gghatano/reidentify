1. recall-1.0 deterministic blocking vs the full cross join
==========================================================================
The block key must be among the attributes the attacker scores on;
otherwise blocking is itself evidence and changes the measurement.

  pairs  full 160,000 | blocked 2,406  (1.50% kept)
  recall 1.0000
  success_analytic  full 0.8950 | blocked 0.8950
  max_risk          full 1.0000 | blocked 1.0000
  top-1 hit rate    full 0.8950 | blocked 0.8950
  baseline random   full 0.0025 | blocked 0.2000

  And the same block key with a score that does NOT use it:
  success_analytic  full 0.1183 | blocked 0.8950   <- blocking added information the score did not have
